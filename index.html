I am a Security Analyst at TraceNetwork, Hyderabad, where I run end-to-end vulnerability assessments and penetration tests for client web applications, APIs and network infrastructure. Each engagement covers reconnaissance, manual validation, exploitation, CVSS-rated reporting and retest after the client fixes the issues.

Before moving to offensive work, I was a SOC Analyst monitoring Splunk and ELK, triaging alerts and investigating incidents. That defensive background shapes how I test: I think about what a finding means for detection and response, not just whether it is exploitable. Lately I also test LLM-powered applications for prompt injection and data leakage, and I am pursuing an M.Tech in Computer Science at CBIT alongside work.
